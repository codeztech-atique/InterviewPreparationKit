# Interview Checklist (one page)

Before the interview
- Environment: ensure editor/CoderPad works, language runtime installed, keyboard and audio tested
- Language: pick your strongest language and stick with it
- Setup snippets: have quick helpers ready (list traversal, tree node class)
- STAR stories: 6 concise metric-driven stories in `STAR_STORIES.md`

During the interview (flow)
- Clarify requirements and ask constraints
- State possible approaches and trade-offs (time/space)
- Choose approach and outline before coding
- Write correct, readable code, covering edge cases
- Run through 3–4 test cases (including edge cases)
- If stuck: state the fallback (working but suboptimal) and ask hints

After coding
- Discuss complexity and possible optimizations
- Mention follow-ups (scalability, concurrency, testing)
- Ask 1–2 thoughtful product/roadmap questions

Quick clarifying questions (examples)
- Can inputs contain null/duplicates/negative values?
- Are we optimizing for time or memory?
- What are expected sizes (n up to?) or streaming constraints?

What to pack for the day
- Charger, quiet headphones, a water bottle
- Printed STAR bullets/metrics (or quick note window)
